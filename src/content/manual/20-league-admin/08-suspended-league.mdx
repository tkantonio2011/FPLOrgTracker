A Super Admin can suspend a league at any time, with an optional reason. Suspension is a deliberate, recoverable state — not a deletion. The data is preserved exactly as it was; only access is gated.

<AnnotatedImage src="suspended-league/banner.svg" caption="The suspended-league banner that replaces every page when a league is in this state." />

## What members see

When your league is suspended, every member who navigates to a `/l/<your-slug>/...` page sees a full-page banner:

> **This league is suspended**
>
> [optional reason from the Super Admin]
>
> Contact your league admin or the platform operator for details.

The actual page content (standings, charts, members table, admin sub-shell) is NOT rendered. It exists in the database; the route handler short-circuits the render at the layout level.

The sidebar still works — they can navigate to other leagues they belong to, or to platform-scoped pages like `/help`. They just can't enter this league's surfaces.

## What you (the League Admin) see

Same banner. Suspension applies to admins too. You can't change settings, invite members, send digests, or read the audit log of a suspended league from the per-league admin sub-shell.

You can still:

- Read the [Audit log](/help/league-admin/audit-log) entry that recorded the suspension, by sorting your league's audit by `league.suspended` — accessible from the platform Super Admin view (visible to them only).
- See the league listed on your [/my-admin](/help/league-admin/overview) page if you administer multiple leagues, with a "Suspended" chip and the deep-link buttons disabled.

## What's preserved

Everything. Memberships, audit events, league settings, digest cache, slug history — none of it is touched by suspension. The state is purely access-control.

When the Super Admin reinstates the league, every surface lights up exactly as it was. No data is lost.

## Why a league might be suspended

The Super Admin's call, but typical reasons:

- The league's mini-league has been deleted on the FPL side, so member sync no longer works.
- The platform operator needs to perform maintenance affecting that specific league.
- The league has become inactive (no logins for months) and is being parked.
- A house-rules dispute that's escalated to the platform level (rare).

The Super Admin can include a public reason that surfaces on the banner. Or they can suspend silently with no reason.

## What to do as an admin

If your league is suspended and you don't know why:

1. **Check the audit log via the Super Admin** — they can see the suspension event including any reason.
2. **Reach out to the Super Admin** — the banner says "contact your league admin or the platform operator". They are the only ones who can reinstate.

If suspension is unexpected, ask first whether reinstatement is straightforward (e.g. you fix the issue that caused it) or whether it's a longer-term decision.

<Callout type="warning" title="Suspension is reversible; deletion is not">
  This app deliberately doesn't expose league deletion as a normal operation — there's no "delete this league" button anywhere. Suspension is the cool-down mechanism. If a league truly needs to go away forever, that's a platform-operator database operation, not an in-app action.
</Callout>

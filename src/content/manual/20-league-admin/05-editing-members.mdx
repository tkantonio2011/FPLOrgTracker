The Members page lists every member of your league with inline edit affordances for each row. Some fields apply to that league only; others propagate to every league the member belongs to.

<AnnotatedImage src="editing-members/modal.png" caption="The edit-member modal showing display name, team name, email, and points-deduction fields." />

## What's editable

### Per-membership (only affects this league)

- **Display name** — what shows in standings, the leaderboard, the digest. Defaults to whatever FPL returns; you can override.
- **Team name** — the FPL team name. Defaults to whatever FPL returns; you can override (e.g. to censor an offensive name).
- **Points deduction per gameweek** — a per-week penalty applied to this member in this league. House-rule mechanism for fining someone who misses a deadline, fails to send a digest, etc. Defaults to 0.
- **Role** — Member or Admin. See [Inviting members](/help/league-admin/inviting-members) for the constraints.
- **Active status** — Active or Inactive. Inactive members don't appear in standings but their data is preserved.

### Cross-membership (affects every league the user is in)

- **Email** — changing the email here changes it on the underlying UserAccount, which affects every league the user is a member of. Be deliberate — see the warning below.

<Callout type="warning" title="Email changes are cross-league">
  Updating a member's email rewires their entire account on the platform. If they're in three leagues, all three will now use the new email for magic-link sign-in. Confirm with the member before changing it, and prefer asking them to update it themselves via their profile (if/when that surface exists) over doing it from admin.
</Callout>

## Removing a member

Two flavours:

- **Soft remove (deactivate)** — flip Active to Inactive. The member disappears from standings but everything is preserved. You can reactivate later. This is the right choice if someone goes on a long break.
- **Hard remove** — actually delete the LeagueMembership row. The member is gone from your league. Their UserAccount and any other league memberships are unaffected. The audit log records the removal.

Hard remove is destructive in the sense that the in-league history (transfers, captain picks per GW) becomes inaccessible from your league's pages. The FPL data is still on FPL's servers — but the app no longer fetches it for this member. Reversing a hard remove is a re-invitation, not a reactivation.

## Points deduction

The points deduction is a per-week subtraction. Set it to 2, and every gameweek for this member is recorded with `actual − 2`. Set it back to 0 to stop deducting.

Common uses:

- A late-deadline penalty applied for the rest of the season ("you missed deadline = -2/wk for 5 weeks").
- A house rule like "if you don't pay your league fee, -1/wk until you do".

Deductions appear as a small badge on the member's standings row and are logged in the audit trail.

## What you CAN'T edit

- The member's FPL Manager ID. Once linked, the Manager ID is the connection to FPL's API; changing it would break their FPL history. If a member changes which FPL team they play, you have to hard-remove and re-invite them with the new Manager ID.
- Anything about their FPL team selections (captain, transfers, chip usage). The app reads those from FPL; you can't write back.
- Their data from other leagues they're in. Each LeagueMembership is separate; this page only edits this league's view.

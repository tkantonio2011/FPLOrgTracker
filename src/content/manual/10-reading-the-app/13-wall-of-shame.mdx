The Wall of Shame is the season's permanent record of the worst moments. Six awards, six trophy cards, one current holder each. The awards update every gameweek as the season progresses — and the awkward truth is that some holders never change, while others trade hands week to week.

<AnnotatedImage src="wall-of-shame/overview.png" caption="The Wall of Shame with six trophy cards. Each card shows the current holder and the specific incident that earned them the title." />

## The six awards

### 🪑 The Bench Warmer

For the most cumulative bench points wasted across the season. This is the slow-burn award — it doesn't change much from week to week, it just slowly compounds. The Bench Warmer is the manager who consistently picks the wrong starting eleven.

### 🩸 The Masochist Medal

For the most points paid in transfer hits across the season. Awarded for *self-inflicted* damage — every -4 you took voluntarily, summed. A high score here means a manager who genuinely cannot leave their team alone.

### 🥄 The Wooden Spoon

For the **lowest single-gameweek score** of the season. Changes hands whenever someone has a truly catastrophic week. Defending the wooden spoon is impressive; reclaiming it from yourself is concerning.

### 🔥 The Bonfire of Vanities

For the **worst single-gameweek bench waste**. Different from the Bench Warmer (which is cumulative) — this is the one week you really should have started the kid from the U23s.

### ©️ The Armband of Doom

For the worst captain blank by raw post-multiplier points. Captain scoring 2 means -8 from the doubled total. Triple Captain scoring 1 is -10. The Armband of Doom is whichever pick stings the most.

### 🤡 The Regret Machine

For the season's single worst transfer. Shows the player sold, the player bought, and the cumulative point delta from the gameweek of the transfer onward. The card surfaces the FULL trade — including the hit cost if any.

## Sidebar — Hall of Repeat Offenders

Below the main grid, a sidebar lists managers who have held two or more awards simultaneously at any point in the season. It's a small honour.

## How awards update

Every gameweek the page recomputes from scratch. There are no "frozen" historic holders — if you got the Wooden Spoon in GW 3 but someone scored lower in GW 17, the title transfers. The page reads from the same data as the analytics pages; updates land within minutes of the previous GW's bonus points finalising.

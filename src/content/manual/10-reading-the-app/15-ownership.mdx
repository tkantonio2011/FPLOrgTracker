The Ownership page lists every player owned by at least one manager in your league, along with how many managers own them. It's the mirror of FPL's global ownership percentages — but scoped to *your* league, which is the comparison that actually matters for league position.

<AnnotatedImage src="ownership/overview.png" caption="The Ownership table. Each row is a player with the count of league owners and the manager names." />

## What the columns mean

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Player** | Name, club, and position. |
| **Owned by** | How many managers in your league own this player out of the total. |
| **%** | Same as Owned by, expressed as a percentage of the league. |
| **Owners** | The list of managers who own them, displayed as chips. |
| **GW pts** | The player's points in the most recent completed gameweek. |
| **Total pts** | Their season total. |

The table is sortable by any column. Default sort is **Owned by descending** — the league's template players are at the top.

## Reading the league's template

The top of the table is your league's "template" — the players most managers own. Owning the template is comfortable; it means you can't fall behind on any given week because everyone else gets the same points from those players.

Below the template, ownership thins out. The middle of the table is where most of the variance happens: players owned by some, not by others. This is where the league is won and lost.

The bottom of the table — players owned by 1 or 2 managers — is the "differentials" zone. See the dedicated [Differentials](/help/reading-the-app/differentials) page for an alternative cut of this data.

## What to do with this information

- **Spot opportunities** — a player on top form whom most of the league DOESN'T own is a transfer-in opportunity for you to gain on the field.
- **Hedge against risk** — a heavily-owned player about to be benched or rotated is a risk for the league but also a chance for you to differentiate by NOT owning them.
- **Plan captaincy** — when most of the league captains the same player, your captaincy choices have less impact. When ownership is fragmented, captain choice swings results.

<Callout type="note" title="Ownership lags transfers by one GW">
  The Ownership page shows ownership *as of the most recently completed gameweek*. Transfers made in the current GW won't reflect here until that GW finalises. Use [Transfers](/help/reading-the-app/transfers) for real-time activity.
</Callout>

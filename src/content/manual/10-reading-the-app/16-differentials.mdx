A differential is a player only some managers own. If they score big, they swing the gameweek. The Differential Alerts page surfaces the differentials that mattered most this gameweek — low-owned players who delivered.

<AnnotatedImage src="differentials/overview.png" caption="The Differentials page — players, their GW points, their league ownership count, and the manager(s) who benefited." />

## What's on the page

The page is a table of differential picks from the most recent completed gameweek, sorted by impact (points × inverse ownership). Each row shows:

- **Player** — name, club, position.
- **GW points** — what they scored in the gameweek (before captain multiplier).
- **Owned by** — count and percentage of league managers who owned them.
- **Captained by** — managers who captained them (these are the big winners).
- **Impact** — composite score combining points and rarity. Higher = bigger differential.

The default filter shows players owned by 30% or fewer of the league. You can raise or lower that threshold with the slider at the top.

## What "impact" means

A 15-point haul from a player owned by 1 of 12 managers is more meaningful than a 15-point haul from a player owned by 11 of 12. The first case is one manager getting a unique 15 points; the second is the whole league getting roughly the same boost.

The Impact column rewards rarity:

```
Impact = GW points × (1 − ownership fraction)
```

So:

- 15 points, 8% ownership → ~13.8 impact
- 15 points, 92% ownership → ~1.2 impact

The same haul, vastly different effect on the league table.

## How to use it

- **Post-mortem** — at the end of a gameweek, scan the top of the table. The managers listed in "Captained by" or "Owned by" are the ones who gained relative ground this week.
- **Transfer planning** — a differential that scored big two weeks running is a candidate for transfer in *before* their ownership rises. The window closes fast.
- **Avoid the herd** — high-impact differentials are easier to spot when ownership is fragmented. If everyone owns the same 11 players, there are no differentials.

<Callout type="tip" title="Watch the captain badge">
  When a manager captains a low-owned differential and it pays off, they often jump multiple rank positions in a single GW. The "Captained by" column flags exactly when this is about to happen.
</Callout>

Season Stats is the dashboard view of the league's collective season so far. It rolls up every gameweek into a single screen of cumulative totals and per-manager breakdowns, so you can see at a glance whose habits have cost them the most.

<AnnotatedImage src="season-stats/overview.png" caption="The Season Stats page with cumulative totals at the top and per-manager breakdowns below." />

## What's on the page

The page splits into three bands:

**League totals** (top): single-number cards summing across every member —

- Total bench points wasted across the season
- Total points paid in transfer hits
- Total captain blanks (captain scored ≤ 3)
- Average gameweek score across all managers, all weeks

**Per-manager breakdown** (middle): each manager's individual contribution to each total, sortable by column.

**Trends** (bottom): cumulative line charts for each metric so you can see whether the league is getting better or worse over time. (Spoiler: usually worse.)

## What each metric counts

- **Bench points wasted** — points scored by your bench players that you didn't get because they weren't in your starting eleven. Autosubs that DO trigger don't count — those are points you got.
- **Transfer hits** — the -4 (or -8, etc.) penalties for transfers above the free allowance. Chip-protected transfers (Wildcard, Free Hit) don't count.
- **Captain blanks** — gameweeks where your captain scored 3 or fewer points (after the multiplier is applied). A failed Triple Captain is especially painful here.
- **Average GW score** — total GW points across all members divided by (members × completed GWs).

## How it relates to the other pages

This page is the **executive summary**. Each metric has a dedicated deep-dive elsewhere:

- Bench detail → [Bench Waste](/help/reading-the-app/bench-waste)
- Transfer detail → [Transfer Regret](/help/reading-the-app/regret)
- Captain detail → [Captain History](/help/reading-the-app/captain-history) and [Captain What-If](/help/reading-the-app/captain-whatif)
- A composite of all three → [Agony Index](/help/reading-the-app/agony)

Use Season Stats to spot anomalies; click through to the dedicated page when you want the story behind the number.

<Callout type="note" title="The numbers refresh with every completed gameweek">
  Season Stats updates as soon as the previous gameweek's data finalises in the FPL API — typically Tuesday afternoon UK time after Monday-night fixtures and bonus-point recalculation finish.
</Callout>

The Form Table re-ranks your league by recent performance rather than season total. Each manager's last three gameweek scores are averaged, and the table sorts by that average — so the manager who's been clobbering it for three GWs surfaces at the top regardless of their overall standing.

<AnnotatedImage src="form-table/overview.png" caption="The Form Table with the trend column. Each row shows a manager's last 3 GW scores, the average, and a sparkline." />

## What each column means

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Form rank** | Position by 3-GW average score. Top = best recent form. |
| **Manager** | Display name + team. |
| **GW-2, GW-1, GW** | The actual scores from the last three completed gameweeks. |
| **Avg (3)** | The average of those three. |
| **Trend** | Inline SVG sparkline connecting the three dots. Green = improving, red = declining, grey = flat. |
| **Overall rank** | Their position in the season-long Standings, for context. |

## How to read the trend

The sparkline does what tables don't: it shows you *direction*. A manager averaging 60 might be 30 → 60 → 90 (sharp recovery) or 90 → 60 → 30 (sharp decline) — same average, totally different stories. The colour of the line tells you which:

- **Green** — the last GW is higher than the first of the three. Momentum is up.
- **Red** — the last GW is lower than the first. Momentum is down.
- **Grey** — flat or near-flat. No clear direction.

The area under the line is subtly tinted to reinforce the direction.

## Why this matters

Total points are the only thing that decides a season, but recent form is what predicts the next few weeks. A manager riding a 90 → 95 → 100 streak is probably making smart choices that will compound; a 60 → 50 → 40 manager is probably leaking points to bench picks and broken captains.

<Callout type="tip" title="Pair this with Captain History">
  If you spot a sudden decline on the Form Table, open [Captain History](/help/reading-the-app/captain-history) for that manager. Bad captain picks for three weeks running are the most common explanation.
</Callout>

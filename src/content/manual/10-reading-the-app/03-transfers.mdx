The Transfers page logs every transfer every member has made this season — player in, player out, cost in points hits, and the gameweek the transfer was made for.

<AnnotatedImage src="transfers/overview.png" caption="Transfers grouped by manager with the most recent moves at the top." />

## What you see

For each manager, a list of transfers in chronological order (newest first):

- **In / Out** — the players bought and sold, with their current price.
- **GW** — the gameweek the transfer was made for. Note: a transfer made on Friday for GW 27 shows GW 27 here, not the calendar week it was made in.
- **Hit** — if the manager spent more than their free transfers, they took a -4 hit per extra transfer. The total cost is shown as `-4`, `-8`, etc.
- **Net result** — combined points scored by the new player(s) over the points the sold player(s) would have scored, minus the hit cost. This is the realisation of the gamble.

## What it tells you

A glance at this page lets you see:

- Who plays aggressively (lots of transfers) vs. set-and-forget (rare moves).
- Who routinely takes hits, and whether those hits paid off (positive net result) or backfired (negative).
- Who panic-traded in to chase last week's points (transferred in a player AFTER their big haul), and whether it cost them.

## Wildcard and Free Hit transfers

Transfers made during a Wildcard or Free Hit are shown but **flagged** as such. The hit cost is zero on those weeks (the chip removes the penalty). Free Hit reverses the next week — the players sold come back and the players bought go away — so the realised value of a Free Hit transfer is unusual to compute and is left off the net-result column for those rows.

<Callout type="tip" title="Use this with Regret">
  Transfers shows you what happened. [Transfer Regret](/help/reading-the-app/regret) ranks the same moves by how badly they aged. Open both side-by-side after a chaotic gameweek.
</Callout>

## Refresh cadence

Transfers data is fetched on a longer cycle than live points — typically every 5 minutes — because it only changes at the gameweek deadline. The "refreshing…" pill appears when the cached value is older than that.

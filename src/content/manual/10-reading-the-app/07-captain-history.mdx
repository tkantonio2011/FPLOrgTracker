The Captain History page is a grid of every captain pick in the league for every completed gameweek. Cell colour tells you the points the captain scored (after multiplier); the most popular pick each gameweek is highlighted.

<AnnotatedImage src="captain-history/overview.png" caption="The Captain History grid — managers on the y-axis, gameweeks on the x-axis." />

## How to read it

- **Columns** are gameweeks (oldest left, current right).
- **Rows** are managers in season-rank order.
- **Cells** show the captain's surname (or shorter form for long names) on a coloured background.
- **Cell colour** scales by post-multiplier points: pale grey ≤ 5, soft green for 6-9, green for 10-15, deep green for 16+.
- **Triple Captain weeks** get a distinctive border so you can spot the gambles.

Hover a cell to see the full player name, the GW number, the raw score, and the multiplier applied.

## What it tells you

- **Cluster columns** — when most of the league captains the same player, you'll see a stripe of identical names. These are the obvious picks (Salah, Haaland, Saka). Differential captains stand out because they break the stripe.
- **Personal patterns** — scan a row and you'll see which managers chase last week's hauler vs. which read fixtures vs. which always go to the captain of the on-form-team.
- **Triple Captain decisions** — the bordered cells are the gambles. Did each manager play their TC on a 30-pointer or a 6-pointer?

<Callout type="tip" title="Use with Captain What-If">
  Captain History shows what each manager did. [Captain What-If](/help/reading-the-app/captain-whatif) shows what they should have done. Open them side by side after every gameweek.
</Callout>

## Captaincy stats card

Above the grid, a summary card surfaces league-wide captaincy facts:

- The most-captained player of the season
- The highest single-GW captain return (player + manager + score)
- The biggest captain blank (player + manager + score)
- The success rate of differential captain picks across the league

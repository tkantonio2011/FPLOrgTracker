The H2H Battle Simulator takes two managers and any single gameweek, then shows the full head-to-head: starting elevens, differential picks, captain choices, and the score line that would have settled it.

<AnnotatedImage src="h2h/overview.png" caption="A head-to-head matchup with the two starting elevens side by side and differential picks highlighted." />

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the two managers">
    Two dropdowns at the top of the page. You can pick any two — including yourself vs. the leader, or two of your rivals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the gameweek">
    Defaults to the most recently completed GW. Use the selector to step back through the season.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    The page renders two columns — one per manager — with their starting elevens, captain pick, bench, chip used (if any), and the final score line at the bottom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's highlighted

**Differentials** — players one manager owns and the other doesn't are marked with a coloured border in the column of whoever owns them. This is where the matchup was actually decided.

**Captain picks** — both captain badges are shown prominently. If they captained the same player, the multipliers cancel; if they captained different players, the captain who outscored the other wins this micro-battle on the page.

**Chip indicators** — Wildcard, Free Hit, Bench Boost, and Triple Captain are all flagged. Note that a Bench Boost extends the starting eleven to 15.

## Why it's useful

- **Post-mortem** — settle "what if we'd played each other this week" arguments definitively.
- **Style comparison** — see at a glance whose teams are riskier (more differentials) vs. more template (mostly the same picks as everyone else).
- **Bragging rights** — if you'd beaten the leader four weeks in a row but had a bad GW 1, this page is your evidence.

## What this page doesn't simulate

It doesn't simulate the FPL H2H scoring format (1 for a draw, 3 for a win across a fixed schedule). This is just total-points head-to-head for the selected gameweek. If your league runs the official H2H format, the standings reflect that elsewhere — this page is a pure points comparison.

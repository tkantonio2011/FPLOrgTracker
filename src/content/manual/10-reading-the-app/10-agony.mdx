The Agony Index is the dark mirror of the Standings page. It ranks managers not by points scored but by points wasted — and weights the different kinds of waste so the leaderboard reflects who has suffered most.

<AnnotatedImage src="agony/overview.png" caption="The Agony Index leaderboard with stacked-bar visualisation showing each manager's pain by component." />

## The formula

```
Agony = Bench points wasted
      + Points paid in transfer hits  
      + Sum of (captain ≤ 3 weeks × 6)
      + Sum of (your GW score below league average)
```

Each component contributes to the total. The mix matters as much as the total — two managers can have the same agony score with completely different stories underneath.

## Reading the stacked bar

Above the leaderboard, the page shows a horizontal stacked bar per manager, with each segment colour-coded by component:

- **Slate** — bench points wasted
- **Amber** — transfer hits
- **Red** — captain blanks
- **Purple** — below-average gameweek suffering

The longest bar is at the top. The legend shows the league-wide total for each component so you can see where your collective league pain comes from.

## Per-manager breakdown

Below the leaderboard, each manager has a card showing:

- Their agony rank and total
- A breakdown of each component
- Their worst single-GW incident in each category — "GW 12 — bench scored 22", "GW 19 — Salah captained for 2 points", "GW 7 — minus 8 transfer hit, gained 1 point"

## What it's good for

- **Identifying habits** — a manager with high bench waste but low transfer hits is a "set-and-forget who picks the wrong starters". A manager with the opposite is a "constant tinkerer". Both bad, both diagnosable.
- **Reading the league's collective pain** — the stacked-bar visualisation tells you whether your league is a transfer-hit-heavy league or a bench-waste-heavy one. Useful for designing house rules.
- **Bragging rights** — being top of the Standings AND bottom of the Agony Index is the cleanest possible season. Being bottom of Standings AND top of Agony is, well, the wooden spoon plus.

<Callout type="note" title="Below-average suffering ≠ a single bad week">
  The "below-average GW suffering" component only counts each GW once per manager. If you scored 60 in a week when the league averaged 65, that's 5 agony points from that week. It's not a cumulative-deficit metric.
</Callout>

The Bench Waste page surfaces the saddest stat in FPL: points your players scored while sitting on your bench, doing nothing for your team total. The page is a heatmap — managers as rows, gameweeks as columns — with each cell shaded by how many points were wasted that week.

<AnnotatedImage src="bench-waste/overview.png" caption="The Bench Waste heatmap. Pale = clean week, deep red = significant waste." />

## How to read the heatmap

- **Cell colour intensity** goes pale gold → orange → deep red as bench points climb.
- **Red outline** on a cell marks that manager's personal worst gameweek of the season.
- **Total column** on the right is each manager's cumulative bench waste so far.
- **Total row** at the bottom is the league's collective bench waste per gameweek. GW columns turn red when the league as a whole left a lot on the bench that week.

Hover any cell to see the exact points wasted, the GW number, and the manager's name. On mobile, tap to reveal the same tooltip.

## What counts as "wasted"

Bench points wasted = points scored by a player on your bench whom you did NOT replace via an autosub. The FPL engine autosubs in a bench player when one of your starting eleven doesn't play (zero minutes); when that happens, those points are *not* wasted — you got them.

So "wasted" specifically means:

- Your bench player scored points
- Your starting eleven all played at least one minute (or were captain/vice and got a captain rule)
- Therefore, no autosub happened, and the bench points didn't count

Bench Boost weeks count zero bench waste — everyone played.

## Why this is the most painful page

Bench waste compounds. Five points missed in GW 1 is annoying; the same five points missed every week is 200 by season's end. The cumulative column tells you the real cost of consistently choosing the wrong starters.

<Callout type="warning" title="The worst bench GWs are not always the worst captain GWs">
  A manager can win a gameweek and still leave 30 points on the bench. Don't confuse "high score" with "well-managed team" — the heatmap will tell you who's getting away with sloppy bench choices on a good week.
</Callout>

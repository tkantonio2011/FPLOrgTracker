FPL Tracker is a private analytics dashboard for your Fantasy Premier League mini-league. It pulls live data from the official FPL API, layers your league's standings on top of it, and unpacks the bits FPL doesn't expose — bench waste, captain regrets, transfer hits, head-to-head comparisons, and a deliberately petty Wall of Shame.

There is no public mode. Every page is gated behind a sign-in, and every member sees only the league(s) they belong to.

## What you can do here

- **Read the league.** Standings, live points, transfers, ownership, form, captain history — every page is one click from the sidebar.
- **Compare yourself.** Head-to-head, captain what-ifs, transfer regret, bench waste — quantify exactly where you went wrong.
- **Track the season.** Cumulative stats, agony index, luck ranking, Wall of Shame trophy cards that update every gameweek.
- **Stay in the loop.** Optional weekly digest email summarises the gameweek with manager-by-manager commentary.

## Where to look first

If you're brand new, the natural reading order is:

1. **[Standings](/help/reading-the-app/standings)** — the home leaderboard. Start here to recognise who's who.
2. **[Live Points](/help/reading-the-app/live-points)** — mid-gameweek tracker. Bookmark it for Saturday afternoons.
3. **[Form Table](/help/reading-the-app/form-table)** — last-three-gameweek momentum. Whose run is real, whose is regression-to-the-mean.
4. **[Bench Waste](/help/reading-the-app/bench-waste)** — the heatmap of cumulative pain.

If you've just been invited as a **League Admin**, the [League Admin overview](/help/league-admin/overview) is your starting point instead.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
  The sidebar is your friend. Sections collapse and expand; the page you're currently on is highlighted in green. The "Help" entry brings you back here.

## Where the data comes from

Standings, live points, transfers, fixtures, player metadata — everything time-sensitive is fetched live from the official Fantasy Premier League API. Your league's roster and admin settings live in this app's database. Both layers refresh on a sensible cadence, and stale data is signalled with a small "refreshing…" pill on the affected page rather than silently leaving yesterday's numbers in place.

## What this app is NOT

It isn't a replacement for the official FPL site — you still pick your team there. It isn't a public social network — no posts, no DMs, no public leaderboards. It isn't betting infrastructure — there's no integration with bookmakers, no odds, no payouts, no money handling.
